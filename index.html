A sustainable approach

Cheap only counts if it lasts. Use this order so savings hold and e-waste, energy use, and risk go down together.

  1. Inventory and tag every asset

    List hardware, licenses, cloud accounts, and contracts with owner, cost, age, and renewal date.

  2. Remove waste before replacing anything

    Switch off idle servers, reclaim unused licenses, and consolidate duplicate tools. This costs almost nothing.

  3. Extend life, then refurbish, then replace

    Move laptops from 3 to 5 years, redeploy good devices to lighter roles, and buy refurbished where warranty is available.

  4. Move to shared and efficient platforms

    Cloud with committed pricing, virtualization, and energy-efficient devices cut both power bills and carbon.

  5. Pilot in one plant or region first

    Prove the saving on a small scale, then copy it. Keep a rollback plan for each change.

  6. Track cost and carbon monthly

    Report cost per user, energy per workload, and devices recycled. Adjust the percentages in the table with real results.

Why this works

Lower running cost

Fewer licenses, less hardware, and smaller support teams cut the bill year after year.

Lower energy and e-waste

Fewer servers and longer device life mean less electricity and fewer discarded devices.

Faster to change

Cloud and mobile tools roll out to new plants and distributors in days, not months.

Less vendor lock-in

Open standards and open-source tools give you leverage at every renewal.
